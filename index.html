A little about me

Curiosity across disciplines. Evidence with purpose.

My professional journey began in nutrition and dietetics and developed into a broader interest in how food systems can support human health while operating within environmental limits. Over time, this interest brought together nutrition science, quantitative research and sustainability analysis.

I hold a PhD in Sustainable Food Systems Modelling from Wageningen University & Research in the Netherlands. My research explored the relationships between dietary choices, nutritional quality and environmental pressures, using quantitative models to investigate complex food system questions.

Alongside academic research, I have worked with international organisations, government partners and the private sector. Experiences with UNICEF, FAO and FrieslandCampina gave me different perspectives on nutrition programmes, scientific communication and the application of evidence in practice.

Today, through QuantSusti, I bring these perspectives together to support organisations with quantitative analysis, evidence synthesis, nutrition and sustainability research, and decision-support tools.

I value work that combines scientific rigour with practical relevance: asking the right questions, making complex information understandable, and helping people use evidence with confidence.
