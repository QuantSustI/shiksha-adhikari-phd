Research & Expertise

Connecting nutrition, environmental sustainability and quantitative analysis.

My research explores how food systems can better support nutritional quality while reducing environmental pressures. I combine evidence synthesis, quantitative modelling and interdisciplinary analysis to understand trade-offs and generate insights that can inform research, policy and practice.

Research Interests

Research Streams

My work brings together nutrition science and sustainability analysis to investigate food systems from complementary perspectives.

01

Sustainable Food Systems

Analysing food system scenarios to explore how dietary choices, food production and resource use influence environmental sustainability and nutritional outcomes.

02

Protein Quality and Nutrition

Investigating protein quality, amino acid availability and the nutritional implications of different food sources and dietary patterns, including the role of protein quality in sustainable diets.

03

Integrated Nutrition and Environmental Assessment

Bringing nutritional indicators together with environmental data to explore potential trade-offs between dietary quality, greenhouse gas emissions and land use.

04

Evidence Synthesis and Nutrition Research

Synthesising scientific evidence on nutrition and health, including systematic review methods and the interpretation of findings for research and decision-making.

Analytical Approach

Methodological Expertise

I use quantitative and qualitative approaches to structure complex questions, assess evidence and translate analytical findings into practical insights.

Quantitative Modelling and Scenario Analysis

Food system modelling, scenario development, sensitivity analysis and comparison of alternative dietary and sustainability pathways using R, Python and GAMS.

Nutrition and Dietary Assessment

Protein quality assessment, amino acid composition, nutritional indicators and interpretation of dietary and nutrition-related evidence.

Environmental Data Analysis

Integrating environmental datasets, including greenhouse gas emissions and land-use indicators, into food system analysis and modelling.

Systematic Reviews and Evidence Synthesis

Reviewing and synthesising scientific literature to identify evidence patterns, research gaps and implications for nutrition and food systems.

Data Processing and Visualisation

Data cleaning, transformation, analytical workflows, reproducible research and visual communication using R, Python and dashboard tools.

Interdisciplinary Research

Integrating perspectives across nutrition, environmental sustainability and food systems, while collaborating with researchers and stakeholders from different disciplines.
